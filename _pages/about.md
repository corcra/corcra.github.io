Hello! I am a Principal Research Manager in Microsoft Research based in Cambridge, UK. I work on artificial intelligence for healthcare, which means I want to build multimodal models of human health and biology, and understand how they work.

My research has spanned a variety of topics in healthcare as well as machine learning, such as [building an early-warning system for circulatory failure in intensive care patients](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-020-0789-4), [drafting radiology reports with a multimodal large language model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04449), and [automating biomarker discovery in histopathology with interpretable deep learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02985). Currently, myself and my team are thinking a lot about building multimodal models of biology as part of [Quine](https://microsoft.github.io/quine/).

I joined Microsoft Research in 2019. Before that, I received a PhD in computational biology and medicine from the [Tri-Institutional PhD Program of Cornell University](https://gradschool.weill.cornell.edu/programs/tri-institutional-phd-programs), during which time I was situated at the Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center in NYC, and later ETH Zürich. Before that, I studied theoretical physics at Trinity College Dublin, and mathematics (Part III) at the University of Cambridge.

Some of my [other interests](http://apeiroto.pe/) include hiking, board games, and watching the wildlife in my garden.
